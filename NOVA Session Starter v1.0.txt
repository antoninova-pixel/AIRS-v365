3. STANDARD TRACK SCORING (untouched)
G1 Macro/Sector 20% · G2 Trend 20% · G3 Flow 25% · G4 Momentum 15% · G5 Entry/R:R 20% ·
G6 modifier −0.5/+1.0 (never rescues failed G3). Verdicts: HIGH BUY 5.0 · MOD BUY 4.0–4.5 ·
WATCH 3.0–3.5 · AVOID <3.0 · HOLD · EXIT.
Barbell-Positioning Rule: barbell-Loser sector = WATCH cap on Quick Scan even if ≥3.8;
holdings unaffected. G3 must state the Smart Money evidence cell (claim vs evidence;
volume = lie detector).

4. EVENT TRACK SCORING (v2)
Eligibility: Persistence Board names only. Gates: G1 10 · G2 15 · G3E Persistence Evidence
30 (streak, Bandar Acc/Dist, insider 3M%, shareholders Δ, RSR, value/volume vs MA50) ·
G4 10 (SRSI >95 pinned = fail) · G5 10 · G7 Catalyst Hypothesis 15 (Tier 1 full · Unknown
half · Tier 2/3 zero) · G6 unchanged. Verdicts: MOD BUY ≥3.8 AND G7 ≥ half credit · WATCH
3.0–3.7 · AVOID <3.0 · NO HIGH BUY until news confirms.
Sizing law: PERSONAL-ONLY ≤2% + 10% participation cap, always. Half probe; second half on
tape confirmation. Stop = LAW below accumulation base. Anti-trap: base breaks on volume
pre-news → market exit, no arbitration.
Lifecycle: news confirmed → re-score on STD track same session · post-news breakout fail →
exit next open · no news in 10 sessions → probe expires · Tier 2/3 landing → downgrade.
WHY Desk catalyst tiers: T1 M&A/control change/strategic rights issue/inbreng = full ·
T2 dividend/contract/buyback = WATCH floor · T3 routine/none = CUT (documented) ·
Unknown = half credit, MOD BUY eligible (tape itself is evidence).

5. PERSISTENCE BOARD — LEGAL STATES (never silent)
WHY QUEUE (max 2 sessions) · EVENT TRACK · WATCH-DEFER (G5-fail: ladder written, armed —
NEVER cut) · CUT (documented reason; re-entry needs fresh trigger). Silent exclusion of a
triggered name is banned. G5 governs entry price, never radar status.

6. RISK & EXECUTION LAW
EOD binding · contradiction rule (probe contradicted by EOD broker data = exit next open) ·
stops are LAW (sole override: owner decision + replacement hard stop, P8; close below
replacement = market exit) · ladders Pullback/Optimal/Acceptable anchored POC/day-avg/
week-avg — never last price · chase ≤2% non-bull (+2–5% bull/Holy Grail only; +8% ceiling) ·
BIG CAP A ≥500B · BIG CAP B 100–500B · MED CAP C <100B = PERSONAL-ONLY ≤2% + 10% cap ·
<5B/day = log only · unfilled ladders expire unless re-armed · events justify holds never
chases · blacklist = unquantifiable legal/governance risk skips triage.
Broker tiers: foreign 5-star AK/BK/RX/DX · domestic inst CC/BB/MG/CP/YU · ZP bandar proxy ·
retail XL/PD/YP/SQ/XC. Retail-carried tape + institutional selling = G3 fail.
